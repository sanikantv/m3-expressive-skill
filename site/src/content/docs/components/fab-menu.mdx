The FAB menu replaces the speed dial. The FAB morphs into a close button, and 2 to 6 labelled
actions open above it. When it closes, the items fold back into the FAB.

<Snippet region="fab-menu" />

## FAB sizes

| Composable | Size | Corner |
|---|---|---|
| `FloatingActionButton` | 56dp | 16dp (`large`) |
| `MediumFloatingActionButton` *(new)* | 80dp | 20dp (`largeIncreased`) |
| `LargeFloatingActionButton` | 96dp | 28dp (`extraLarge`) |

<Snippet region="medium-fab" />

## Accessibility

- The toggle FAB comes first in traversal order (`traversalIndex = -1f`).
- It announces Expanded / Collapsed.
- Back closes the menu.

Google's sample also wraps the FAB in a `TooltipBox` and adds a "Close menu" custom action on the
last item.

## Common mistakes

- Putting more than 6 actions in the menu. Move extra actions to a sheet.
- Leaving the FAB visible while the list scrolls. Use `Modifier.animateFloatingActionButton(visible = …)`.
