Floating toolbars replace the bottom app bar for contextual actions. In Google's before/after,
a docked bar with a FAB becomes a floating pill toolbar with a separate FAB of a different shape
and color.

<Snippet region="floating-toolbar" />

Colors: `vibrantFloatingToolbarColors()` (primary container) or `standardFloatingToolbarColors()`
(surface container). To collapse a toolbar without a FAB to its main content, use the overload
with `expanded`, `leadingContent` and `trailingContent`.

## Common mistakes

- Placing it inside `Scaffold(bottomBar = …)`. It floats over content: align it bottom-centre in a `Box`, offset by `FloatingToolbarDefaults.ScreenOffset`.
- Forgetting `Modifier.nestedScroll(exitAlways)` on the scrolling parent, which stops it hiding on scroll.
