`SegmentedListItem` gives settings screens the Expressive look: list items inside one rounded
group, with larger corners on the first and last items. It has overloads for plain, `onClick`,
`selected` and `checked` items.

<Snippet region="segmented-list" />

The `Switch` has `onCheckedChange = null` because the whole row is the toggle. That gives one touch target and one screen-reader announcement.
