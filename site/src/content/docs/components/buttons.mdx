## Shape morph on press

`Button` has a new overload that takes `shapes: ButtonShapes`. Pass `ButtonDefaults.shapes()` and
the button is round at rest and squarer while pressed, animated by the theme's fast spatial
spring. Without `shapes` you get the old fixed-shape button.

<Snippet region="button-shapes" />

`FilledTonalButton`, `ElevatedButton`, `OutlinedButton` and `TextButton` have the same overload.

## Five sizes

| Size | Height | Icon | Constant |
|---|---|---|---|
| Extra small | 32dp | 20dp | `ButtonDefaults.ExtraSmallContainerHeight` |
| Small (default) | 40dp | 20dp | `ButtonDefaults.MinHeight` |
| Medium | 56dp | 24dp | `ButtonDefaults.MediumContainerHeight` |
| Large | 96dp | 32dp | `ButtonDefaults.LargeContainerHeight` |
| Extra large | 136dp | 40dp | `ButtonDefaults.ExtraLargeContainerHeight` |

For anything other than the default, size every part from the same height with the `…For(height)` helpers:

## When to use which

- **One** medium or large button for the main action of a screen, like "Start free trial" on a paywall.
- Default size for everything else. Two large buttons side by side compete for attention.

## Common mistakes

- Setting `Modifier.height(56.dp)` without `shapesFor`, `contentPaddingFor` and `textStyleFor`. The label and corners then stay sized for 40dp.
- Passing `shape =` and expecting the press morph. The morph only comes with `shapes =`.

## Accessibility

Text buttons need no extra semantics. Icon-only buttons belong in [icon buttons](/components/icon-buttons/).
