A split button pairs a main action ("Send", "Save") with a menu of related actions. The trailing
half is a toggle. While it's checked, rotate the arrow and show your `DropdownMenu`.

<Snippet region="split-button" />

Variants come in matching pairs: `LeadingButton`/`TrailingButton` (filled), `TonalLeadingButton`/`TonalTrailingButton`,
`ElevatedLeadingButton`/`ElevatedTrailingButton` and `OutlinedLeadingButton`/`OutlinedTrailingButton`. Don't mix them.

## Accessibility

The trailing button is icon-only. Give it a content description ("More save options") and a
`stateDescription` of Expanded/Collapsed.
