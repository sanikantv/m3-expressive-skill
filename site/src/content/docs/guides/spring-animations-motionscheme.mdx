**Short answer:** read `MaterialTheme.motionScheme` and pass one of its six specs as the
`animationSpec`:
- `defaultSpatialSpec()`, `fastSpatialSpec()` or `slowSpatialSpec()` for size, position, corners
  and morphs
- `…EffectsSpec()` for color and alpha

With `MotionScheme.expressive()` the spatial specs bounce slightly. The effects specs never do.

## Why not tween?

A tween has a fixed duration and curve. When someone taps again mid-animation it either restarts
or jumps. A spring keeps its velocity and retargets smoothly. Material components already use
the theme's springs, so a custom `tween(300)` next to them looks out of place.
