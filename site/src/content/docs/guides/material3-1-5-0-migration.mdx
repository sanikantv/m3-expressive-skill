**Short answer:** the most common warnings after upgrading to 1.5.0-beta02 come from four APIs:
- `ListItem(headlineContent = …)` → put the headline in the trailing `content` lambda
- the old `SearchBar`/`DockedSearchBar` → `SearchBar(state, inputField)` with an expanded search bar
- `TopSearchBar` → `AppBarWithSearch`
- non-state `Slider` overloads → `Slider(state, …)`
