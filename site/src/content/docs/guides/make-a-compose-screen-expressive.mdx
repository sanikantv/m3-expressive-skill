**Short answer:** find the one action and the one piece of information that matter most on the
screen. Make them stand out with shape, color and emphasized type. Group the rest. Turn
selections into shape changes, and animate with springs. Then stop.

Google's research behind M3 Expressive found people spotted key UI elements up to four times
faster on expressive screens. That only works if *one* thing stands out.

## The checklist

1. **Find the primary action and the key fact.** One of each.
2. **Make the action bigger, with a contrasting shape and strong color.** For example, a medium
   button, or a square-ish FAB next to a pill toolbar.
3. **Emphasize the key fact.** Use `titleLargeEmphasized` or `displaySmallEmphasized`, and save
   the huge display styles for hero screens.
4. **Group the rest** in `surfaceContainer*` containers. The most important group gets the
   brightest one.
5. **Turn selection into shape:** connected `ToggleButton`s, `SegmentedListItem` selected states.
6. **Morph instead of swapping**, and use `MaterialTheme.motionScheme` springs.
7. **Stop and test:** light, dark, two dynamic color wallpapers, font scale 200%.

## Hero moments: one or two per product

Google's guidance is one or two hero moments per **product**, not per screen. Examples are a
paywall, an onboarding finish or a "goal reached" screen. Everywhere else, keep it calm.
