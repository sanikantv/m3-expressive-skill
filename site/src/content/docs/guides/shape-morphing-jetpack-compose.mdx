**Short answer:** build a `Morph(MaterialShapes.Circle, MaterialShapes.Cookie9Sided)` once,
animate a 0..1 progress with a spatial spring, and draw `morph.toPath(progress)` in a small custom
`Shape`. `MaterialShapes` and `toPath` still need `@OptIn(ExperimentalMaterial3ExpressiveApi::class)`.

<Snippet region="morph-shape" />

<Snippet region="morph-toggle" />

`Morph` comes from `androidx.graphics:graphics-shapes`. Add it if your project doesn't pull it in
already.
