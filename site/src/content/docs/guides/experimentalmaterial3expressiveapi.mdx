**Short answer:** in material3 **1.5.0-beta02** you need
`@OptIn(ExperimentalMaterial3ExpressiveApi::class)` only for:

- `LoadingIndicator`, `ContainedLoadingIndicator`, `LoadingIndicatorDefaults`
- `MaterialShapes` with `RoundedPolygon.toShape()`, `toPath()`, `Morph.toPath()`
- the checkable `DropdownMenuItem(checked, onCheckedChange, text, shapes, …)` overload
- `PullToRefreshDefaults.LoadingIndicator`, and one `Slider` thumb overload

Everything else in Expressive is stable. The same list applies to beta01.

## Opt in only where you need to

<Snippet region="loading-indicator" />

A file-level `@file:OptIn(ExperimentalMaterial3ExpressiveApi::class)` is fine in files that use
`MaterialShapes` a lot. Avoid project-wide opt-ins in Gradle: they hide which calls are still
unstable when you upgrade.

## Coming from an alpha?

In the 1.5.0 alphas most of Expressive was experimental, and some names changed between alphas.
After upgrading, delete opt-ins one file at a time and let the compiler show you which ones are
still needed.
